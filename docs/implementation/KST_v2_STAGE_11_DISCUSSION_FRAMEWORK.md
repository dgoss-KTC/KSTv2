## 2. Draft projection sketch — not accepted

The following is a vocabulary sketch, **not a business rule or implementation instruction**.

```text
as-of boundary
    opening supply S0(component, approved inventory boundary)
    + accepted supply events dated in bucket t
    - accepted demand events dated in bucket t
    = projected balance B(t)

B(t0) = S0 + supply(t0) - demand(t0)
B(t)  = B(previous bucket) + supply(t) - demand(t), for later buckets

future shortage in t = max(0, -B(t)) only after the owner accepts
  source populations, dates, units, event sequence, shared-inventory boundary,
  and unknown-data behavior.
```

The sketch deliberately does not select an inventory quantity, demand date, supply receipt date,
same-day ordering, netting boundary, UOM conversion, safety-stock policy, or late-supply treatment.
It cannot identify a “covering PO” or “projected clear week” until those decisions are accepted.

### 11-A accepted direction — workspace report shape

The first Stage 11 surface is a distinct **Long-Term Shortages** tab in the existing workspace navigation,
alongside Dashboard, Planning, Component Orders, Finished Goods, and the locked Stage 9 **Shortages** tab. It reports the
full active workspace—not only work orders selected in a prior view. The report range begins in the
current week and displays exactly 24 weekly buckets, with the current week as week 1. The range
uses the existing Sunday-start MPS/scheduler calendar through the end of the 24th displayed week, inclusive.
The projection is date-only: its as-of value is the report refresh date, and it has no timezone
concept, timestamp precision, or user-facing timezone setting.

The legacy KST v1 shortage report is accepted as a **format reference only**. Its final shortage
sheet is component-level, with component context, an optional first-short/status/manufacturer
presentation, demand attribution, PO context, on-hand context, and a sequence of projected weekly
balance columns. It also separates PO and parent/component usage detail. Stage 11-A may retain
that layered presentation after the projection contract is accepted; it must not copy the legacy
report's broad filters, all source pulls, Excel machinery, calculations, or implied semantics.

Initial workspace options belong at the top of the Long-Term Shortages page rather than in a
standalone tools area. Candidate controls include the existing/QAD-style choices for manufactured
parts, phantoms, and additional columns. Each control remains an owner decision until its precise
meaning, default, eligible population, and effect on report calculations are accepted. A future
outside-workspace tools/report runner is explicitly deferred and must not drive the workspace
contract.

### 11-B direction — single-part MRP

Single-part MRP is a distinct follow-on slice. It will let a user investigate one explicitly chosen
part against an accepted time-phased fact set and will reuse 11-A facts only where their business
meaning and scope fit. It is not an alternate presentation of a workspace report, and it is not
authorized until 11-A establishes the shared time, balance, and provenance rules.
